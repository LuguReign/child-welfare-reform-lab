# Qualitative support and mixed-method learning

Planning materials only: no interviews have been conducted and no quotations or themes are presented as findings. Interviews with children or families require an appropriate ethical protocol, consent/assent process, safeguarding, confidentiality and qualified facilitation. Use approved materials and voluntary participation; do not collect unnecessary identifiable details.

## Semi-structured questions for staff or monitoring partners

1. Describe how a visit is recorded, approved, corrected and included in an extract.
2. Where can completed visits be absent or misclassified in the system?
3. What operational barriers affect timely, meaningful visits?
4. How do placement transitions or staffing changes affect continuity?
5. Which measure exclusions are most difficult to interpret consistently?
6. What would children and families say these measures miss?
7. Which changes are feasible, and what evidence would show improvement?

## Initial codebook (hypotheses, not findings)

| Code | Include | Distinguish from |
|---|---|---|
| Documentation gap | Recording delay or extract loss | Actual service not delivered |
| Capacity barrier | Staffing, caseload, travel constraints | Individual blame without corroboration |
| Definition ambiguity | Conflicting eligibility interpretation | Missing data with clear definition |
| Continuity disruption | Transitions breaking relationships | General workload |
| Family-defined quality | Perspectives on meaningful support | Merely documenting a contact |
| Improvement proposal | Specific action and intended mechanism | Unsupported claim of success |

Pilot the codebook on approved notes; independently double-code a subset, discuss disagreements and record revisions. Preserve contradictory cases rather than forcing consensus. Summaries should identify source type, sampling limits, missing voices and confidence.

## Mixed-method synthesis template

Quantitative signal → data quality caveat → corroborating/contradicting qualitative evidence → tentative explanation → alternative explanation → next evidence needed → action owner.

Example **hypothesis only**: a decline in documented visit coverage could reflect recording delays rather than fewer visits. Test with extract control totals, approved case review and staff interviews before recommending operational changes. Include families' experience before equating contact frequency with support quality.
